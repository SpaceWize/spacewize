Services

What I actually do

Most projects are a full design-and-build: we talk, I design it, I build it, it goes live. If you already have a site and it just needs to work better, that's fine too.

Design & build

A complete site from a blank page. Structure, layout, copy direction, and a working build you own outright. Typically three to six pages, launched in under a week.

Redesign

You have a site. It's slow, dated, or nobody can find anything. I audit what's there, keep what works, and rebuild the rest without losing your search rankings.

Ongoing care

Once you're live I can keep the site looked after - on-call help, updates and backups, checks and broken link fixes, seasonal updates if you want them, and notes on what changed. $160 CAD a month, cancel any time. There's no limit on what I can change, unless it needs different pricing.

Add-ons

Extras for a redesign or new build

Each of these can be added to a redesign or a new build for a slightly higher quote.

  • Account systemNot managed by me unless you have On-Call Site Care.
