About

Six divisions in one company run by one person might sound like a lot — only one is live, and two more are waiting.

There's a reason there's so many, and a reason it's a cherry tree. Cherry blossoms are the strongest representation of life I know: they bloom, they're beautiful, and they're gone within two weeks, maybe less. Think of the blossoms as opportunities and ideas. I'm cultivating them to get to my desire for freedom, peace and more time.

A bit about me

Started this site at 15. Thought of the business since 12.
I like anime, being with my family, and making money while building cool things.

This isn't just going to be a multi-branched business. It's the plan. Everything I do gets its own site, and they all stay connected under one umbrella brand — owned and run by the same person, on purpose.

What's actually running

Web Development is live already, and there's work to show for it — a rebuild for the nonprofit AWDEA, a site for consultant and event host Ace Spaders, and a class site for Computer U Teach, with a few more on the way.

See the site.

Why the others aren't yet

3D Printing opens in spring 2027, and Dropshipping in summer of the same year. The other three don't have dates yet — they're further off, and they open once the first three are live and stable, so I don't get overloaded and each one gets built properly.

Real Estate is last for a purpose. It's the one that I want the most but can do the least, financially, legally, and with knowledge. So I'm building my stepping stones to get there. I love houses, and believe home is where your life is and where you build the most memories with people you love, so it's literally the most important part of your life.

Each division is a stepping stone. I finish one, keep it running, and start the next — all the way to Real Estate, which comes last. Once that's live, Space Wize Enterprise is a fully functioning business. The goal isn't just for me: from websites to toys to homes to jobs, I want each division to do something good for someone while I build my own future doing it.

Where this is

BC, Canada. Remote worldwide.
