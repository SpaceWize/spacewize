Six divisions in one company run by one person might sound like a lot, but notice, only one is live, and two more are waiting.

This is because its not just going to be a multi-branched business.
It's because its my plan.
I built this page so that everything I do has a site, and it all stays connected in some degree. Being owned and run by the same person, and them all connecting to one umbrella brand. And i have a deliberate purpose to it.

What's actually running

Web Development is live already, and has work to show for it. I've done a few versions of a site for non-profit AWDEA, a website for solo consultant/advisor/business partner and event host Ace Spaders, a computer and coding class Cpmouter U teach, and a few more are coming soon.

See the site.

Why the others aren't yet

The 3D printing line blooms in the spring of 2027, and the Dropshipping one in the summer of the same year. The other three don't have a date yet, because they are still a little far off.

THey open up when the first three divisions are open, budded and stable. This is to ensure I don't get overloaded, and that the flowers bloom nicely.

A bit more description about them: They are, to me, stepping stones towards my goals. I complete one division, it keeps runnging and I start up the other one. This keeps going until the Real estate Branch, which comes last, is live. Then I consider Space Wize Enterprise a fully functioning business.
My Goal for this isn't just for me. I want it to help people in all sorts of different ways. From making websites, to selling toys, to building homes, to creating jobs in the process all in between, I want this to do something good for everyone, and build my own future while I'm at it.
