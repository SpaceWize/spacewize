Services

What I actually do

Most projects are a full design-and-build: we talk, I design it, I build it, it goes live. If you already have a site and it just needs to work better, that's fine too.

Design & build

A complete site from a blank page. Structure, layout, copy direction, and a working build you own outright. Typically three to six pages, launched in one to two weeks.

Redesign

You have a site. It's slow, dated, or nobody can find anything. I audit what's there, keep what works, and rebuild the rest without losing your search rankings.

Ongoing care

Once you're live I can keep the site looked after - updates, backups, small edits, and someone to call when something breaks. $100 CAD a month, cancel any time. For bigger changes than that, it's a new project - see pricing below.
